Where To Go

Howe Sound is the whole point.

North America's southernmost fjord starts at the Squamish waterfront. Granite walls, glassy morning water, and shorelines you simply cannot reach by road. These are the classic runs from the Squamish launch:

Britannia Beach

A short run south past Watts Point — mining-museum shoreline, mellow water on most mornings, and a good first-leg confidence builder.

Porteau Cove

Marine park, dive site, and one of the prettiest stretches of the Sound. Sunset here off the water is a Sea to Sky rite of passage.

Anvil Island

The big triangular landmark mid-Sound. Quiet bays, seal haul-outs, and eagles working the cliffs — bring the long lens.

The No-Road Coves

Dozens of beaches and coves between Squamish and Lions Bay have no trail and no road. A boat is the only ticket in.

Good To Know

Boat rental FAQ

Where can I rent a boat in Squamish?

Squamish Water Taxi rents an 18-foot inflatable RIB with a 50 HP engine, departing from Squamish on Howe Sound. It’s a bareboat rental — you skipper it yourself. Book online or call (604) 849-8898.

Do I need a license to rent a boat in Squamish?

For a motorized boat, yes — Canadian law requires proof of boating competency, most commonly a Pleasure Craft Operator Card (PCOC) or a recognized equivalent. No license is needed for canoes, kayaks, or paddleboards, and none is needed on skippered trips where the captain drives.

No license? No problem.

Two easy routes onto the water: book a skippered water taxi trip or tour where a licensed captain does the driving, or rent a canoe, kayak, or paddleboard from Squamish Canoe Rental — paddle craft need no license at all.

How much does a boat rental cost?

RIB rates vary by season and rental length — check live availability when you book online or call (604) 849-8898 for a quote. Skippered wildlife tours start at $375 for up to 3 guests. Canoe and paddleboard rates are listed at squamishcanoerental.com.

What’s included with the RIB rental?

Life jackets for everyone aboard, navigation tools, all Transport Canada required safety equipment, and a local orientation covering conditions, fuel, and recommended routes before you head out.

Is this good for water sports?

Squamish is one of the best water sports towns in BC — paddling the lakes, exploring the Sound, kiteboarding off the Spit. If your day involves getting a boat, a board, or a paddle in the water, one of the three options above covers it.
